    About the data

    A static snapshot, built from Wikipedia.

    The comparison uses outdoor national records published on English Wikipedia. Each result links back to its source page so you can inspect the underlying record.

    Times are refreshed during a dedicated TypeScript data build, then shipped with the site. Your browser makes no data requests and your entered time never leaves the page.
